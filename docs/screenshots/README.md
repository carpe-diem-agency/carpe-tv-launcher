# Screenshots

The install-page Home and Settings previews were captured from the isolated
non-debuggable Carpe TV 0.2.44 (51) build on MiTV-AFMU1 at 1920×1080 and
scaled to 1280×720. Home shows the new default blue wallpaper and the
launcher’s initial favorites. Settings shows Appearance & sound with the
blue wallpaper selected. The captures exclude personal account screens,
device identifiers and the installed-app catalog.

The comparison also uses `docs/assets/google-tv-home-test-stick.png`, a real
1920×1080 capture of the stock Google TV Home on the same MiTV-AFMU1. It shows
the owner's app row and third-party recommendation artwork; it is labeled as
Google TV in both languages and is not presented as a generic mockup. Review
the visible account avatar and recommendation imagery again before publishing.

`docs/assets/interface-preview.mp4` is a 10-second H.264 screen recording of
the app library, adding AirReceiver to favorites, and moving a Home tile on
the same Xiaomi stick. The transitions were triggered by ADB input with HDMI
output off; it is not a physical-remote or projector smoothness test. The
library scene shows app names installed by the owner, including personal apps;
review those frames before broader promotion. Video has no microphone audio.

Replace and review these preview assets from the final production-signed
release candidate before publishing the first stable release. Check visual
scaling on the connected projector and remove device-specific content.
